Volumetric Planner

Apply client coefficients to shipment data and see volumetric load by client, hub or route.

● Works offline · data never leaves this computer

Transportation master sheet

Upload once (CSV or Excel). Needs Destination and Lane Code columns; Lane Origin is used if present. Each next_location is matched to a Destination and its volumetric is added to that Lane Code. Saved on this computer; upload again to replace it.

Client coefficients sheet

Upload once (CSV or Excel with Client and Coefficient columns). Saved on this computer; upload again to replace it.

Route mapping sheet

Upload once (CSV or Excel). Uses OD Pair, origin_dc, Via1–Via6, destination_dc and Next Location. Large files are kept in this browser's database. Upload again to replace it.

Vehicle capacity sheet

Upload once (CSV or Excel). Uses Fleet Type and Capacity. Fleet names are matched ignoring case, spaces and underscores. Saved on this computer; upload again to replace it.
