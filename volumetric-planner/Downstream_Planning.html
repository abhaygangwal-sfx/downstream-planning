Downstream Planning

● Works offline · data never leaves this computer

Transportation master sheet

Upload once (CSV or Excel). Needs Destination and Lane Code columns; Lane Origin is used if present. Each next_location is matched to a Destination and its volumetric is added to that Lane Code. Saved on this computer; upload again to replace it.

Client coefficients sheet

Upload once (CSV or Excel with Client and Coefficient columns). Saved on this computer; upload again to replace it.

Route mapping sheet

Upload once (CSV or Excel). Uses OD Pair, origin_dc, Via1–Via6, destination_dc and Next Location. Large files are kept in this browser's database. Upload again to replace it.

Vehicle capacity sheet

Upload once (CSV or Excel). Uses Fleet Type and Capacity. Fleet names are matched ignoring case, spaces and underscores. Saved on this computer; upload again to replace it.

1 Floor pendency

Upload the shipment export (CSV or Excel). The client_name column is matched to your client coefficients.

2 LH Intransit

Upload one source or both (CSV or Excel). When both are uploaded, their rows are added together. Adds OD Pair (Origin hub + destination column) and Next Loc (route mapping's Next Location for that OD Pair, otherwise the destination column). Existing columns are kept as they are.

A: LH Intransit — Data from Ecom DC Floor Pendency
Destination: Shipment_Destination_dc · Client: Client
B: LH Intransit — Data from LH Incoming Vehicle Visibility Dashboard
Destination: Shipment_Final_Destination_dc · Client: client_name

3 Warehouse Pendency

Upload the warehouse pendency export (CSV or Excel). client_name is matched to your client coefficients. destination_dc is matched to the destination column of the transportation master to get the lane. Lanes follow the selected origin hub.

4 FM Load

Upload the export (CSV or Excel). Only rows where origin_dc and origin_bagging_dc both equal the selected Origin hub are processed; other rows are excluded before any calculation. client is matched to your client coefficients. Destination_dc is matched to the destination column of the transportation master to get the lane.
